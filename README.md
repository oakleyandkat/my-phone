# My Phone — a life sim you play through a phone

A cute pastel life-simulator played on a simulated phone screen.
Lock screen (passcode **1234**) → sakura home screen → apps.

## What's in it
- **My Day** — wake up, closet with 21 outfit items, walk to the desk, pick up the phone
- A drivable car to get between places
- First-person 3D places: office, grocery, cafe, boutique, pharmacy, home & gifts, salon, Jess's move

## How to run it
Open `index.html` in a browser. That's it — no build step, no npm, no server.
Three.js and every scene are inlined in the one file.

## Put it on your phone
Open the live link on your phone, then:
- **iPhone (Safari):** Share icon → Add to Home Screen
- **Android (Chrome):** ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen

It opens fullscreen like a real app, with a pink cherry-blossom ✿ icon.

## Tips
- Hold the phone upright (portrait) — it's designed as a phone-shaped screen.
- In 3D places: drag to look around, joystick (bottom-left on mobile) to walk, tap things to interact.
- If a 3D scene looks frozen, back out and re-enter it.
